Lumino is a STEAM learning adventure platform for learners, parents and guardians. Its workshop challenges bring science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics to life and help learners build the technical skills to put creative ideas to work. A planned resource center will help learners revisit what they’ve learned and give families ways to keep learning together.

Explore the prototype

Start with five subjects

Winston helps you find a starting point in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology or English. Each subject has a short explanation, a workshop challenge and a chance to recall what you learned.

Try ideas and improve them

Future challenges will ask you to imagine several designs, test one and explain what you would change. Design, storytelling and communication help you show who your solution is for and how they can use it.

We want learners to use the same judgment with AI suggestions. Ask what a suggestion assumes, check it against evidence and decide how to improve it.
